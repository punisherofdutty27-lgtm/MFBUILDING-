Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between a veranda and a pergola?

A veranda is an enclosed, glazed room usable all year; a pergola is an outdoor structure, open or with adjustable slats, that creates shade. We build both.

Does a veranda need a permit?

Often yes, depending on the municipality and the area. We guide you on the process, but the permit remains your responsibility.

How is a veranda kept watertight?

Careful treatment of the junctions with the building and of the roofing is essential. That's exactly our waterproofing specialty.

Do you offer bioclimatic pergolas?

Yes, with adjustable slats to control shade and ventilation. We advise the right solution for your outdoor space.
