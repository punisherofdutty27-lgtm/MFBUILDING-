A flat roof that stays watertight

On a flat roof, water doesn't drain as quickly as on a pitched roof: the smallest weakness in the membrane eventually causes infiltration. We work on all types of roof terraces, whether accessible or not, to install reliable waterproofing suited to your substrate.

Our roof waterproofing services

  • Full renovation of flat roof waterproofing
  • Installation of bituminous or synthetic membranes (PVC, EPDM)
  • Liquid (resin) waterproofing for complex surfaces
  • Treatment of upstands, joints and detail points
  • Associated thermal insulation (warm / inverted roof)
  • Maintenance and periodic waterproofing checks

How we choose the right system

The right build-up depends on the substrate, the slope, how the roof is used and the existing insulation. We carry out an on-site diagnosis, then a clear quote stating the chosen solution and a firm price. Every project is covered by a written guarantee.

Frequently asked questions

How much does flat roof waterproofing renovation cost in Geneva?

It depends on the area, the type of membrane and the condition of the substrate. We provide a free, detailed quote with a firm price after an on-site diagnosis.

Which waterproofing membrane should I choose for a flat roof?

Bitumen, synthetic membrane (PVC, EPDM) or liquid resin: the choice depends on the substrate, slope and use. We advise you on the most durable solution.

Do you respond urgently in case of infiltration?

Yes, we prioritise a fast response to limit damage, then propose a durable repair or renovation.

Is your waterproofing work guaranteed?

Yes, our work comes with a written guarantee provided at handover.
